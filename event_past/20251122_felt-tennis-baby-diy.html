[免费] 手作活动！DIY 毛毡网球宝宝

FREE! Felt Tennis Baby DIY Workshop

A relaxing and creative hands-on activity where participants made adorable felt jellycat tennis baby accessories

活动概述 / Event Overview

最近被这个奶乎乎、软萌到爆的毛毡网球宝宝疯狂治愈！于是我们成功举办了这场轻松又充满创意的手作活动，一起做了一个属于自己的网球宝宝！🥹💚

We successfully hosted this relaxing and creative hands-on activity! Participants joined us to make adorable felt jellycat tennis baby accessories. The event brought together craft lovers of all skill levels for an afternoon of fun and creativity.

活动亮点 / Highlights from the Workshop

  • 专业指导 / Expert Instruction: Step-by-step guidance from experienced craft instructors
  • 创意设计 / Creative Designs: Each participant crafted their own unique felt tennis baby with personalized details
  • 成品展示 / Showcase: Participants proudly displaying their adorable handmade creations
  • 轻松氛围 / Relaxing Atmosphere: A warm and welcoming environment perfect for unwinding and getting creative
  • 材料齐全 / Materials Provided: All crafting supplies including felt, stuffing, and embellishments included

社区影响 / Impact & Community

This workshop brought together families and craft enthusiasts, fostering community connections through shared creativity. Participants not only took home a charming handmade keepsake but also made new friends and discovered a love for felt crafting. The event reinforced EvoAFuture's commitment to providing joyful, accessible community activities for everyone.

活动地点 / Location Details

The workshop was held at 1754 Junction Ave, San Jose, CA 95112, providing a comfortable and well-equipped space perfect for crafting activities.
